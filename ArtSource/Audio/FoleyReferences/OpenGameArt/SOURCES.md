The upload pages identify the CC0 licenses. Source archives and extracted
recordings are retained locally and ignored by Git, without importing them into
Assets. These credits remain versioned. Local audition manifests record the exact
source files used by candidates.

Skeleton audition edits select short dry fracture textures and adjust speed,
bandwidth, fades, and level. Zombie edits trim two recorded nonverbal vocalizations
and lightly adjust speed, bandwidth, and level. Slime textures exclude the source
impact onsets and overlap the remaining wet/slurping bodies into periodic, smoothly
leveled movement loops. These Slime loops contain no authored footfall envelopes.

Approved Skeleton hurt and death edits use the dry crunch sources. The wet Slime
movement and recorded Zombie voice auditions were rejected; Slime movement is
silent and the final Zombie hurt derives from the approved synthesized demon
hurt instead. Rejected auditions stay local; installed clips are under `Assets/Audio/`.
