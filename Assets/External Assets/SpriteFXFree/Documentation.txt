Sprite FX Free

Documentation. 6 2D sprite shaders, ported from the Godot pack: the same effects, settings
and defaults, for SpriteRenderers.

  Resources/Sprite FX Free/   the shaders, one per effect
  Scripts/SpriteFX.cs   Apply, Clear, Set, Animate; Flash, Blink, DissolveOut/In
  Demo/SpriteFXFreeDemo.unity   every effect on the Godot demo's sprites; click one to hit it. Its code
                         is in Scripts/Demo/, its sprites in DemoArt/

Use:
  SpriteFX.Flash(sprite);                              // hit flash, then the own material is back
  yield return SpriteFX.DissolveOut(enemy).Wait();     // then Destroy(enemy.gameObject)
  SpriteFX.Apply(coin, "shine");
  SpriteFX.Apply(slime, "outline").Set("outline_color", Color.yellow).Set("width", 2f);
  SpriteFX.Clear(coin);                                // the own material back

Apply puts an effect on a renderer, on a material of its own, keeping the renderer's material for
Clear; one effect at a time, a second replaces the first. Or give a SpriteRenderer a material of a
"Sprite FX Free/..." shader yourself: every setting is in its inspector.

Effects (SpriteFX.Effects lists them): flash, outline, dissolve, shine, hue_shift, blink.

Settings: Set("progress", 0.5f), Set("edge_color", Color.cyan), Get("width"): the shader's properties,
by the Godot pack's names ("flash_amount") or the shader's ("FlashAmount", "_FlashAmount"). Sizes
are in texture pixels, as in Godot. Animate(property, from, to, seconds) runs one and, unless told
not to, gives the own material back after; Finished and Wait() tell when.

The effects read the sprite as a texture of its own: import sprites with Mesh Type Full Rect, not
from an atlas, and give an outline or glow room to draw in, transparent pixels round the art (or the effect's Grow).

Needs Unity 6.3 or later, in any render pipeline: the shaders are unlit sprite shaders, tested in
Built-in, URP (2D and Universal Renderer) and HDRP. The demo runs on either input handler.
