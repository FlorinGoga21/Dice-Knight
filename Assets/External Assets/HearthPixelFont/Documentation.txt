Hearth

Documentation. 1 pixel font, each as a TextMesh Pro font asset made from its bitmap
(point filtered, so crisp) and as TTF.

  Hearth/Hearth TMP.asset   crisp at font size 8 and its multiples
  <Font>/<Font>.ttf        the outline font, for a dynamic TMP asset of your own
  <Font>/BMFont/           the bitmap font the TMP asset is made of
  Demo/HearthPixelFontDemo.unity   every font at 1x to 4x; type your own text to try it

Keep the text on a canvas scaled by whole numbers (PixelCanvas does it) and at whole-pixel positions.

Needs Unity 6.3 or later and TextMesh Pro's Essential Resources (Window > TextMeshPro > Import
TMP Essential Resources; Unity offers it when a TMP text first shows).
